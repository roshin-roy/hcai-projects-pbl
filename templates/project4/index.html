Preference Elicitation for Movie Recommendation

This project compares two ways of collecting movie preferences. In one task, you choose between two movies. In the other, you rank ten movies from the one you would most like to watch to the one you would least like to watch.

Your answers are used to estimate a preference model for each method. The report explains the movie features, the models, and the proposed study procedure.
