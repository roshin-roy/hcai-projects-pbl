Before you begin

This is a research study on how people express movie preferences. You will complete two kinds of tasks:

  • Pairwise choice: you see two movies and pick the one you'd rather watch.
  • Ranking: you see ten movies and order them from most to least preferred.

Your responses are stored for the current study session and are used to estimate your movie preferences at the end. Participation is voluntary and you may stop at any time.
